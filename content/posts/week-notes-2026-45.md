This week was quite busy. Worked on Bloom, the org-mode android app, a job application tracker and got some reading done. 

# Bloom
I did a lot of work on Bloom but I had to scrap about 80% of what I did, because I was making it too complicated. I worked on adding dir support to Bloom. This was quite the work. I had to scrap almost 3 days of work and a lot of code because it ended up becoming too confusing and complicated 

Similarly, I thought of adding Seafile support as a way of adding support for external storage providers. I thought this would be easy and straightforward enough since I just had to write adapters for external providers like Seafile. This is because Seafile connects to the android filesystem via SAF APIs. But Seafile, especially their Android app had so many quirks that addressing each of them tended to make the codebase more convoluted and messy. Ended up scrapping about 4 days of work in an attempt to step away from it for now, so that I can come back with fresh eyes and design a better solution.
