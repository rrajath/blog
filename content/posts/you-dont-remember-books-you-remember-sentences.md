> You don't remember books, you remember sentences 

I heard this in the Psychology of Money podcast ([episode](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/six-quotes-that-changed-me/id1675310669?i=1000777220751)) and it resonated with me. 

I always wondered and got frustrated that I don't remember the books I read. But when I heard this quote, it made me realize that it's not the books we remember, it's the ideas in them. Especially the ones that strike a chord when we read/hear them. And that may come from a chapter, a paragraph or even one sentence. 

I used to try out strategies for remembering what I read only to fail at it later. But the fact was, if it resonates with me I'd remember. It's as simple as that. This doesn't mean that I wouldn't make notes. I still would. But I won't worry about not remembering things.
